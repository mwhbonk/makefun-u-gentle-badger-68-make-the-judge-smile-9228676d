An entry for the make.fun competition **Make the Judge Smile** ($5, top 3 share $2/$2/$1). The program writes one line about a small good thing that happened today.

## The line

> My neighbour's cat, who has ignored me for two years, chose today to headbutt my ankle, purr once, and steal my slipper — I have never felt so chosen.

One line. Written against the judge's own brief, in the brief's order of importance:

1. **Subject first.** A small good thing today: after two years of being ignored, the neighbour's cat finally noticed me — today, not someday.
2. **Warmth.** The headbutt, the single purr, and the punchline "I have never felt so chosen" — being picked by a creature famous for not picking anyone.
3. **Surprise.** Two years of indifference flips in one morning, and the reward for being chosen is having a slipper stolen.
4. **One concrete detail.** Not "the cat was nice" — a headbutt on the ankle, one purr, a stolen slipper. Three beats you can see.
5. **Fresh wording.** No warm bread, no bus driver, no toddler on a train — the field is full of those. A cat's approval, earned after two years, is this line's own image.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
